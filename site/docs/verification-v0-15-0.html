Its 13 focused commits include Sharath K's authored model-adapter contribution, integrating RFC #14 and PR #17. Limits

Controlled model HTTP servers verify dialect behavior, not hosted-provider interoperability or inference quality. Sharath's Ollama 0.40 report remains attributed evidence, not a rerun. Token enforcement depends on the remote server; reported excess is detected after completion. Bedrock/Vertex signing, Azure-specific authentication, streaming and embeddings remain planned. Local executable runtime checks used Linux AMD64; other platforms rely on native CI and archive inspection. AHP remains experimental. OS-native signing, notarization, GPG Helm signatures and hosted package-channel repositories remain outside this release. Historical releases and their records retain their scope.
